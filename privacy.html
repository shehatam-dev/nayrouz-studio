Privacy policy

Nayrouz Studio Publisher · Effective November 9, 2026

What this covers

Nayrouz Studio Publisher is a Meta app run by Nayrouz Studio, a trade name of Shehata Tech Solutions LLC. Its only job is to publish Nayrouz Studio's own posts to our own Facebook Page and our own Instagram account. Nobody else can sign in to it or connect an account to it.

What information the app uses

The app uses an access token for our own Facebook Page and Instagram account. With it, the app publishes posts and reads the posts already on our Page and account, so it does not publish the same post twice.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone who views, likes, comments on or shares our posts. It does not read comments, messages or followers.

Where information is kept

The only records the app keeps are the links to the posts it published and its own run logs. Those are held in our private GitHub account and contain no information about other people.

Shopping with us

Purchases are made on Etsy, not through this app. Etsy's privacy policy covers what Etsy collects when you buy: etsy.com/legal/privacy.

Data deletion

This app stores no data about Facebook or Instagram users, so there is nothing about you to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, email shop.coptic@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will tell you what, if anything, we hold, and delete it within 30 days.

You can also remove any app's access to your own Facebook account under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
